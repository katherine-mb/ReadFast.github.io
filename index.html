Task Management Application

Welcome to the Task Management Application

In this Task Management Application, you are able to manage your tasks easily and locally.

To get started, click below:

Manage Your Tasks

Features

Add, Edit and Delete Tasks

Create new tasks that you need to complete. Edit their name, description, and status.

Filter and Sort Your Tasks

Easily filter and sort your tasks based on due dates, priority, name or status. Also have the option to search for specific tasks.

Task Analytic Dashboard

Have a small dashboard that visualizes task data. It displays a bar chart showing you the completed vs your pending tasks.
