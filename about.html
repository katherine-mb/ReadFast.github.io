About Us:

Have you ever wanted to have a true library of your physical TBR? Now you can!

With ReadFast you can upload your copy of any book by inserting the title, author, publishing year, pages and a tag of whether you have read it or want to read it.

You can use our page calculator where you enter your book pages and the amount of time you would prefer to finish your book for a custom calculation of the recommended ppd (pages per day).

Try our reading speed test where you can find how long it takes you to read each page for personalized calculations.
